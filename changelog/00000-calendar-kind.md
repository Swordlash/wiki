## New features

- "New page" offers a third kind of page, Calendar, beside a document and
  a presentation. A calendar's page shows its own text, then its days:
  the pages directly under it whose title is a date, newest first, each a
  link to the day's page. To add a day, choose "New subpage" on the
  calendar, give it the date as its title and start it from the template
  "Day". A calendar "Team days" with three pages under it shows:

  ```text
  Pages under "Team days"     The calendar lists
  2026-11-25                  Days
  2026-11-27                    Friday, 27 November 2026
  Notes                         Wednesday, 25 November 2026
  ```

  "Notes" is not a date, so it is not listed. A calendar with no days yet
  says how to add one.

- The calendar is a built-in plugin. You can turn it off in Settings,
  under "Plugins", and the administrator can turn it off for the whole
  wiki. While it is off, "New page" does not offer calendars, and a
  calendar's page shows only its text, as a document does.
