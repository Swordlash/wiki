## Bug fixes

- In the editor, with the search open (Ctrl+F), the grey "Draft saved"
  that shows a moment after typing stops, and the green "Saved" after a
  save, were drawn over the search's ↑, ↓ and × buttons. While the
  search is open, the two notices now show under the search's panel,
  at its right end, and the buttons stay in sight. With the search
  closed they show under the editor's toolbar, as before.
