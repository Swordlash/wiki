## Other changes

- The writing assistant is now the wiki's second built-in plugin. Its
  buttons over the editor's text, its panel and its settings come from
  the plugin, through the wiki's interface for plugins, and look and work
  exactly as before: the same buttons, the same chat, the same tracked
  changes to accept or reject, and the same settings page with its keys
  and passkeys.
