## Other changes

- The wiki now builds on a newer haskell-halogen. The tests that mount a
  view of the wiki without a browser use the library's own waits, which
  give up after ten seconds by themselves and read what the view alone is
  doing, in place of the wiki's copies of them. Nothing on the wiki's
  pages changes.
