## Other changes

- A step of the settings' sections "Security" and "Writing assistant" that
  makes, unwraps or proves a key (making or unlocking your keys, adding a
  way to unlock them, adding a passkey, a second factor, an e-mail address,
  an API key) no longer leaves a key in the page when you leave the section
  before it ends. Its keys are held for that step alone, and go when the
  section closes, or when you sign out. Nothing on the wiki's pages changes.
