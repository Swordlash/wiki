## Other changes

- The wiki has the first part of an interface for plugins, and a list of
  the plugins built into it. The editor's marks for the formulas, diagrams
  and TikZ pictures that cannot be drawn now come from the first built-in
  plugin, "Errors of formulas and diagrams". Every plugin is on, and
  nothing on the wiki's pages changes: the same problems are marked and
  counted, at the same moments, and an island still shows its error in
  place.
